# Kawal Asap

Kawal Asap provides reviewed information about fires and haze. These terms
separate source observations from public claims.

## Language

**Satellite detection**:
A thermal observation supplied by a satellite data product. It is not a confirmed
fire or a count of separate fires.
_Avoid_: Confirmed fire, incident count

**Field report**:
A submitted account of conditions at a place and time. Submission alone does not
make the account approved for publication.
_Avoid_: Verified evidence, publication

**Private original**:
A source file kept for review under the agreed consent and retention rules. It is
not a public attachment.
_Avoid_: Public copy

**Public copy**:
A reviewed file approved for public use. It can differ from the private original
to protect people or remove private information.
_Avoid_: Private original

**Publication**:
A reviewed revision approved for public access, with its sources and reuse terms.
_Avoid_: Submission, raw report

**Withdrawal**:
An editorial decision to end public access to a publication or public copy.
Copies that readers have already obtained cannot be recalled.
_Avoid_: Deletion of all evidence

**Guidance**:
Reviewed advice for a stated audience, with source references and a review date.
_Avoid_: Personalized medical advice
